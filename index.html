About

I am a graduate student in probability at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Beijing Normal University, advised by Professor Mei Zhang.

My current research concerns branching processes in random environments and the asymptotic behavior of their martingale limits. My broader interests include stochastic analysis and random fields.

Preprints

2026 · arXiv:2610.25848

Left tail asymptotics for the density of supercritical branching processes in random environment

Panyi Chen and Mei Zhang

We study the martingale limit of a supercritical branching process in a random environment. Under suitable moment, boundedness, and non-lattice assumptions, we establish a continuous annealed density and precise power-law asymptotics near zero for both the distribution function and the density.
